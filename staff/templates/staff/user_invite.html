How it works

  1. Paste your staff emails or upload a CSV.
  2. Pick a role and (optionally) an organisation.
  3. Preview shows exactly who is new, already registered, or invalid.
  4. Confirm to create them all. Each can be emailed a link to set their password.

Accounts are created as active and verified. They are never given Django admin rights.
