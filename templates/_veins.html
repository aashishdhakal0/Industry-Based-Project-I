{% comment %} The circuit-vein backdrop for the account screens. Entirely original, entirely CSS/SVG — no image, nothing fetched, so there's no licence to worry about and nothing for CSP to block. How the light travels: each vein is drawn twice. Once as a dim static trace, so the circuitry is always faintly there; once more on top with a long dash-gap pattern whose stroke-dashoffset animates. The visible dash is a short segment of bright stroke that slides along the path — a pulse of light running down a vein. Vary the duration and delay per vein and they stop looking like a mechanism and start looking like a system doing something. The geometry is deliberately half circuit, half anatomy: 45° trace bends like a PCB, but the long runs curve. Straight-only reads as a wiring diagram; curves-only reads as lava. The mix is what makes it read as veins of energy. Nothing here is announced to a screen reader — it is atmosphere, and there is no sentence that could usefully describe it. {% endcomment %}
