About

Cyber training that treats you like an adult, not an IT department.

{{ site_name }} teaches Australian small businesses, councils and schools how to stay safe online — in plain English, without assuming you know what a firewall is.

Who it's for

The people who end up responsible for cyber security without ever choosing to be. The office manager who handles the invoices. The council officer with the shared inbox. The school administrator whose password opens more doors than anyone realises.

None of them are technical. All of them are targets — and most existing training is written as though they've already got a security background, which is why it doesn't stick.

What makes it different

Most training tells you that you scored 55% and leaves you there. Knowing you failed isn't the same as knowing what to do next.

{{ site_name }} looks at every question you got wrong, works out which lesson covers the thing you actually misunderstood, and builds you a personal study plan from it. Not a score — a short, specific list of what to revisit and roughly how long it'll take. That's the whole point of the platform.

How we keep it simple

Every screen has one obvious next action. Lessons are about ten minutes. Modules unlock in order, so you're never staring at a menu wondering where to start. There's no jargon, and where a technical term is genuinely unavoidable, we explain it in the sentence where it first appears.

You practise in safe simulations, where a wrong click costs nothing and teaches something. Far better to make the mistake here than in your real inbox.

Who's behind it

{{ site_name }} is built for AUSDAIS PTY LTD, based in Bundoora, Melbourne. It's made in Australia, for Australian organisations, using examples that reflect what actually turns up in Australian inboxes.
