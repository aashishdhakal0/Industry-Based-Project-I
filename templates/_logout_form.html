{% comment %} Logout is a POST, not a link. A GET logout is a CSRF hole in miniature: any site can embed and sign our users out as they browse. Harmless-looking, endlessly annoying, and trivially avoided. Django's own LogoutView has required POST since 4.1 for the same reason. Styled as a quiet button so it reads as a link despite being a form. {% endcomment %}
