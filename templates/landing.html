How it works

Three steps, repeated for each module.

01

Learn

Concise lessons in plain English, grounded in the threats Australian organisations face day to day — not abstract theory.

02

Practise

Apply what you've learned in a safe simulation, where a wrong decision costs nothing and teaches something.

03

Earn your certificate

Pass each quiz to earn a certificate worth sharing with your team, your board, or your auditor.

Rewards

Progress you can actually see

Training only counts if it's finished. Every lesson advances something you can see, which makes momentum easy to keep.

  • Points for every lesson. Ten per lesson, fifty for each quiz passed — progress you can measure.
  • Levels that reflect real progress. The ring shows exactly how close the next one is.
  • Streaks that reward consistency. Short, regular sessions outperform the occasional marathon.
  • Milestones worth working towards. Locked achievements stay visible, so there's always a clear next goal.
