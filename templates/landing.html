How it works

Three steps. You can finish a whole module on your lunch break.

01

Learn

Short lessons in plain English, built around the scams that actually turn up in Australian inboxes — not textbook theory.

02

Practise

Try it in a safe simulation and see what happens. Far better to make the mistake here than in your real inbox.

03

Earn your certificate

Pass the quizzes and get a certificate you can share with your team, your board, or your auditor.

Rewards

Progress you can actually see

Training people abandon is training that changed nothing. So every lesson moves something visible.

  • Points for every lesson. Ten a lesson, fifty for a quiz you pass. They add up faster than you'd think.
  • Levels that mean something. The ring fills as you go, so you always know how close the next one is.
  • Streaks for coming back. A little and often beats one long slog — and your streak keeps score.
  • Badges worth earning. You can see the locked ones, so you always know what's next.
